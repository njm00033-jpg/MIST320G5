# HEW Talent Matching Project Scope Statement

## Product Scope Description
The HEW Talent Matching system will provide a platform that helps connect students with employers based on student qualifications and employer needs. Students will be able to create profiles containing information such as their skills, certifications, projects, experience, and courses. Employers will be able to create job postings that include job information, requirements, and required skills.

The system will compare student information with job requirements to help recommend jobs to students and potential candidates to employers. Students will also be able to identify skill gaps between their current qualifications and the requirements of jobs they are interested in. The system will also support interview scheduling and provide placement analytics for administrators.

## Project Deliverables
The project will include the following deliverables:

- Student profile management
- Employer job posting management
- Talent matching between students and job opportunities
- Skill-gap analysis
- Interview scheduling
- Placement and matching analytics
- GitHub project backlog containing user stories and acceptance criteria
- Project documentation

## Acceptance Criteria
The project will be considered successful when:

- Students can create and update their profiles.
- Students can add skills, certifications, projects, and experience.
- Employers can create and manage job postings.
- Employers can specify required skills for job postings.
- The system can compare student qualifications with job requirements.
- Students can view relevant job recommendations.
- Employers can view potential student matches.
- Students can view skill gaps for employment opportunities.
- Students and employers can manage interview requests.
- Administrators can view placement and matching information.
- The major system features function according to their defined user stories and Definition of Done criteria.

## Project Exclusions
The following items are outside the scope of this project:

- The system will not make final hiring decisions for employers.
- The system will not guarantee employment or placement for students.
- The system will not replace an employer's existing human resources system.
- The system will not process payroll or employee benefits.
- The system will not provide full online course or certification programs.
- The system will not conduct interviews directly through the platform.

## Project Constraints
The project has the following constraints:

- Development must be completed within the time available for the course.
- The project must be completed using the technologies and resources available to the development team.
- Development responsibilities must be divided among the members of the project team.
- The project must follow the requirements established for the course.
- Features may need to be prioritized because of development time and team size.
- The system depends on accurate information being entered by students and employers.

## Project Assumptions
The project is based on the following assumptions:

- Students will provide accurate information about their skills, experience, and qualifications.
- Employers will provide accurate job descriptions and requirements.
- Users will have access to an internet connection and a device capable of accessing the system.
- Students and employers will maintain their information as it changes.
- Administrators will have access to the information needed to review placement and matching results.
- The development team will have access to the software and tools required to build and test the system.
