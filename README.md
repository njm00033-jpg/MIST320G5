# HEW Talent Matching

## Project Overview

HEW University currently has difficulty efficiently connecting more than 12,000 students with hiring partners. Job postings are placed on static bulletin boards, while student resumes are manually reviewed by a limited number of career coaches. This process can result in students being matched with jobs that do not fit their skills and employers receiving candidates who do not meet their needs.

The HEW Talent Matching project will develop a Talent Analytics and Job Matching Platform that improves the process of connecting students with employers. The system will use student profile information, completed courses, project portfolios, and skills to match students with relevant job opportunities.

The platform will also help identify skill gaps for students, support interview scheduling, and provide university administrators with information about job placement and student success.

## Project Scope & Objectives

### Project Scope

The project will focus on designing and developing a platform that improves how HEW University students are connected with employment opportunities.

The system will include the ability to:

- Store and manage student profile information
- Track student skills, courses, and project experience
- Store job postings from corporate partners
- Compare student skills with job requirements
- Recommend relevant job opportunities to students
- Identify skill gaps between students and job requirements
- Assist with interview scheduling
- Provide administrators with job placement analytics

The initial project will focus on the core features needed to demonstrate the talent matching process. Additional features may be added later depending on project time and available resources.

### Project Objectives

The main objectives of the project are to:

1. Improve the process of matching students with relevant job opportunities.
2. Reduce the amount of manual resume review required by career services staff.
3. Help employers identify students who meet their job requirements.
4. Give students better visibility into the skills required for different positions.
5. Provide students with personalized information about their skill gaps.
6. Provide HEW University administrators with useful job placement and success analytics.
7. Create a system that can support HEW University's large student population.

## Team Roles & Governance

The HEW Talent Matching project team consists of four members with separate professional roles and responsibilities.

The team will use GitHub Projects to organize tasks, monitor project progress, and assign responsibilities. Major project decisions will be discussed by the team before implementation. The Product Manager will manage product priorities and ensure that development stays aligned with the project's objectives.

The Scrum Master will help organize the team's workflow and ensure that tasks continue moving forward. The Lead Business Analyst will focus on understanding business and user requirements. The Systems Architect will focus on the technical structure and design of the system.

Team members will communicate regularly about their progress and any problems that could affect the project schedule. When major decisions are required, the team will discuss available options and determine the best approach for the project.

## RACI Matrix

RACI represents four levels of involvement:

- **R - Responsible:** Performs the work required for the task.
- **A - Accountable:** Ultimately responsible for making sure the task is completed.
- **C - Consulted:** Provides input or expertise for the task.
- **I - Informed:** Kept updated about the task and its progress.
